# Search

The console's front page: the tinytrader mark and a box to search a ticker. As you type, a menu narrows the tickers the console knows (every EDGAR listing, earnings calendar companies, the relays' symbols, symbols in recent news): exact first, then by symbol, then by name.

**Data:** stock snapshots, the options screener and news search (alpaca-data); earnings from the economic calendar service.

**Using it:**

- Pick a ticker (↑↓ and Enter, or a click): its results take the panel's place. The price and the day's change; where the screener covers it, its sector, market value, week and month changes, 30-day implied volatility, IV over realized, the expected move and volume against its average; its next earnings; and recent news.
- **Chart** and **Options** point the view's Candles or Spread builder at it, opening one if the view has none. **Add to watchlist…** adds it to a [watchlist](watchlist.md). **Open as a view** makes a view of its own for it (chart, news and earnings).
- ‹ goes back to the search box. The tab is named for the ticker shown, and keeps it across reloads.
- The header's search box shows its results in the view's Search panel, or in a new one beside the active panel.
