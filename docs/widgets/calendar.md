# Calendar

The market month, Monday to Friday: trading days, early closes and holidays, with scheduled economic releases, FOMC decisions and earnings as layers. Resting the mouse on a day (0.7 s) shows its card, so passing over the month never covers it; clicking shows it at once and pins it. A hard click (Safari on a Force Touch trackpad), a double-click or a long press opens the day hour by hour in a [Day](day.md) tab beside it.

**Data:** The market calendar from the first service serving one; releases and earnings from the economic calendar service.

**Using it:**

- The layers shown (Econ, Earnings, Oil) follow the workspace theme.
- One line on top: the month with ‹ › and Today, whether the market is open, and the layers to show (Econ, Earnings, Oil).
- The grid fills its panel; when the rows get short, each day's tags become a dot (hover or click the day for its card).
