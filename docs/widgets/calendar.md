# Calendar

The market month: trading days, early closes and holidays, with scheduled economic releases, FOMC decisions and earnings as layers. Hovering a day shows its card; clicking pins it.

**Data:** The market calendar from the first service serving one; releases and earnings from the economic calendar service.

**Using it:**

- The layers shown (Econ, Earnings, Oil) follow the workspace theme.
- It sizes itself to its tile, so it reads as a page or in a narrow column.
