# Positions and risk

Live accounts' positions and orders, as on [Accounts](accounts.md) but without the equity chart, with total equity and value at risk above them.

**Value at risk:** one day at 95%: each position's market value × its underlying's 60-day realised volatility × 1.645 / √252, summed without offsets. Options count at market value, so short options are understated.
