# Earnings

Upcoming reports with what options expect against what has happened before, to judge whether the expected move is rich or cheap: the jump implied by the term structure, the straddle, and the company's past moves, EPS and surprises.

**Data:** Report dates and times from the economic calendar service (Nasdaq, Finnhub and Alpha Vantage compared); histories from SEC EDGAR and Alpha Vantage; option chains from alpaca-data.

**Using it:**

- Reports are split into before the open, after the close and not disclosed.
