# News

Recent stories, newest first, filtered by symbol with the chips along the top.

**Data:** Every service streaming news (Benzinga, via Alpaca, plus Bloomberg's RSS headlines every five minutes, which carry no symbols); in a themed view, also recent stories on the theme's symbols from alpaca-data's news search, and the same for the watchlist picked.

**Using it:**

- The Theme chip shows stories on the theme's symbols or mentioning its keywords.
- The **Watchlist** chip shows stories on the symbols of the list picked in the [Watchlist](watchlist.md) widget, and follows it when another is picked. In a view without a theme it's chosen first.
