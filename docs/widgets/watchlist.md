# Watchlist

A list of symbols with price and the day's change, refreshed every few seconds while shown. There can be as many lists as you like (nuclear, oil, value…), kept in this browser.

**Data:** Stock snapshots from a service that serves them (alpaca-data), at IEX prices.

**Using it:**

- Pick a list from the menu. A first visit starts with Core, Mega caps, Nuclear, Oil, Value and Semis, to edit or delete. In a view with a theme, the theme's symbols are a list too ("This view's theme").
- **+** makes a list, **✎** renames it and **×** deletes it (asked first). Type symbols into the box to add them, several at once (`CCJ, VST`); × on a row removes one.
- Click a symbol to make it the one the Candles and IV heatmap panels in the view follow. When a list is shown (the view opening, or another list picked), they start on its first symbol, unless they're already on one of its symbols. Clicking a symbol also narrows the view's [News](news.md) to its stories; clicking it again goes back to the list's. Once a row is clicked (or the table tabbed to), ↑ and ↓ step through the list the same way: chart and news follow. Click **%** to sort by the day's change (again for the other way, a third time for the list's order).
- Each Watchlist panel keeps its own list, so views (in a Watchlists folder, say) can each show one. The [News](news.md) widget in the view follows it, with its own **Watchlist** chip.
- The dot after a symbol sets [alerts](alerts.md) on it: a price to rise or fall to, or a move on the day in percent. It's filled when the symbol has alerts (orange once one has fired).
- **Watchlists**, a folder in the market folder (Market, or Markets if yours is called that), holds a view for each list, named after it: the watchlist set to that list over a chart of the symbol clicked, with the list's news beside it. ⌥↑/⌥↓ step through them once inside it (⌥→ on the folder). Moved elsewhere, it stays where you put it and new lists' views follow it. A list made later gets its view; a list renamed takes its view's name along; a view deleted doesn't come back.
