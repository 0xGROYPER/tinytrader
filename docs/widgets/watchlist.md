# Watchlist

A list of symbols with price and the day's change, refreshed every few seconds while shown. There can be as many lists as you like (nuclear, oil, value…), kept in this browser.

**Data:** Stock snapshots from a service that serves them (alpaca-data), at IEX prices.

**Using it:**

- Pick a list from the menu. A first visit starts with Core, Mega caps, Nuclear, Oil, Value and Semis, to edit or delete. In a view with a theme, the theme's symbols are a list too ("This view's theme").
- **+** makes a list, **✎** renames it and **×** deletes it (asked first). Type symbols into the box to add them, several at once (`CCJ, VST`); × on a row removes one.
- Click a symbol to make it the one the Candles and IV heatmap panels in the view follow. Click **%** to sort by the day's change (again for the other way, a third time for the list's order).
- The [News](news.md) widget follows the list picked, with its own **Watchlist** chip.
- The ready-made **Watchlist** view (in Market) puts a watchlist over a chart of the symbol clicked, with news on the list beside it.
