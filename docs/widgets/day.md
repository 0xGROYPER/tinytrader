# Day

One market day hour by hour, in New York time within 04:00 to 20:00 and only as far as something happens (from the first hour with a release, the open, earnings or now, to the last), with three or more quiet hours in a row folded to one line: the pre-market, the session and after-hours shaded apart, each scheduled release at its time, and the day's earnings where they land.

**Data:** releases and earnings from the economic calendar service (its merged calendars); the session's hours from the market calendar.

**Using it:**

- Open it from the [Calendar](calendar.md): a hard click on a day (Safari on a Force Touch trackpad), a double-click, or a long press on a phone. It opens as a tab beside the calendar, on that day; opening another day reuses the tab.
- Earnings before the open sit at the end of the pre-market and those after the close at the start of after-hours, largest companies first, each with its market value and EPS estimate (or result). Reports whose time isn't given are listed under the hours. The workspace theme's symbols are underlined.
- Click a symbol to point the workspace's charts (Candles, IV heatmap) at it.
- ‹ and › step a day, and Today returns; the tab keeps its day across reloads. On today, a red line marks the time.
- It can also be added like any widget, from the palette (⌘K); it then opens on today.
