# Earnings week

The week's earnings reports as a grid, after Earnings Whispers' weekly chart: Monday to Friday across, each day split into before the open and after the close (and a row for reports whose time isn't given).

**Data:** the economic calendar service's merged earnings calendars (Nasdaq, Finnhub and Alpha Vantage), with each company's market capitalisation and EPS estimate.

**Using it:**

- Tickers are ordered by market capitalisation, largest first, and the largest are drawn bigger. The workspace theme's symbols are outlined; a dashed outline means the calendars disagree on the date.
- Hover a ticker for the company, its size and the EPS estimate. Click it to point the workspace's charts (Candles, IV heatmap) at it.
- ‹ and › step through the weeks; This week returns. Companies of $50B and over get a tile (up to 8 a half-day); the rest follow as badges, 24 at most before "+N".
