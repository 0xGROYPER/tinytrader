# Spread builder

An options position from a structure, or suggested for a stock picked in the Options screener, priced from the live chain, drawn on Candles, and sent as an order to a paper account.

**Data:** a service serving option chains (alpaca-data): quotes from Alpaca's indicative feed, with implied volatility computed by alpaca-data from each mid (Alpaca's own values are not used). Refreshed every 30 seconds.

**Using it:**

- Type an underlying in the ticker box, or click a ticker in another widget (Earnings week). Pick the expiry; each shows the move priced into it.
- **From the screener:** clicking a row opens the builder (beside the screener if it isn't in the workspace) with a suggested position and why (`lib/recommend.js`). Options priced rich against realized volatility (IV over realized at 1.15× or more), or a high-IV or expected-move view, suggest an iron condor: shorts at the 16th and 84th percentiles of the expiry's implied distribution, wings near the 4th and 96th. Priced cheap (0.85× or less): an at-the-money long straddle. On the Put skew view, a skew of 2 points or more either way: a credit spread on the rich side (short near the 25th or 75th percentile, long near the 8th or 92nd). The expiry is the one nearest 30 days, between 14 and 60, with a usable distribution where there is one; an earnings report before it is pointed out. Strikes sold have a bid, strikes bought an ask. It's a starting point: adjust it like any other.
- Pick a structure (iron condor, iron fly, strangle, straddle, call or put spread, single call or put) and a **width** in listed strikes: legs are placed from the at-the-money strike outward.
- The legs are a positions table like the broker's: each contract's symbol, mid price (bid × ask beneath), signed quantity (negative sells), market value and delta in shares, with the net in the last row. Step a strike with ‹ ›, switch call or put with C/P, step the quantity with − / + (through zero it flips between buy and sell), remove a leg with ×, or add one. The structure becomes Custom.
- One line sums it up: the price at the mid and crossing the spread, maximum profit and loss, breakevens, and the chance of profit at expiry, per position of 100 shares a contract. The chance comes from the expiry's implied distribution, fitted to the whole chain with its skew and fat tails ([the method](../implied-distribution.md)), when the fit is good or rough; for a chain too thin to fit, from a lognormal at the at-the-money volatility. It says which.
- **Open on chart** draws the position on [Candles](candles.md), points it at the underlying, and opens Candles if it isn't in the workspace. Legs are changed here, not on the chart.
- **Order ticket:** pick a paper account, a quantity and the limit (net per spread, positive pays, negative receives; it starts at the mid), then **Review order**, which lists every contract, the most paid or received and the maximum loss, and **Send order**. It goes to that account's order gateway as one combo order (one order for a single leg), day, opening, as the console's trade client `console` (ids `tt-`), where the gateway's caps and kill switch apply ([order gateway](../../services/order-gateway/README.md)). The answer is shown as the gateway gives it: accepted (not a fill), rejected, not sent, or unknown (it may have reached the broker: don't resend; it shows under working orders if it did). The console's working orders on every paper account are listed with **Cancel**, refreshed every 10 seconds. Without the console's trade token configured, the ticket says so and sends nothing.
- **The live account** (real money) is in the account list too, through its own gateway with small caps ($1,000 an order, 2 contracts a leg, 4 open orders, 20 submits a day). It's never the default: a paper account is. Its ticket is marked live, and **Send live order** stays disabled until you type the account's name; the console also refuses a live order the ticket didn't confirm. Flatten and Resume aren't offered for it.
