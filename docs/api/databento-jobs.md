# databento-jobs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Buy historic DBN from Databento's batch API, with a quoted price and an exact confirmation before any charge, then download each finished job and file it in the [store](store.md). |
| **Transport** | HTTP/1.1, JSON, on `JOBS_LISTEN` (default `127.0.0.1:9121`). |
| **Versioning** | Unversioned paths; additive changes only. |
| **Auth** | Writes are refused from a web page of another origin; bind to loopback or a private network. A charge needs a person's approval through the [console](console.md): the bearer token `JOBS_APPROVE_TOKEN`, `approved_by`, and `confirm_usd` equal to the quoted price. Free requests need none of it. It writes to the store with `JOBS_STORE_TOKEN` (or `_FILE`). |
| **Errors** | `{"error": "..."}`: `400` invalid request, `403` a paid purchase without approval, `409` stale, changed, declined or still-pricing quote, or nothing left to buy, `428` confirmation needed, `503` no ledger configured, or what's had can't be read in full, `502` Databento failed or quoted a price that isn't a number of dollars. |
| **Health** | [Observe](observe.md) endpoints and `/events`. `idle` while it yields to live capture. |
| **Stability** | **Experimental.** |

## Configuration

| Variable | |
|---|---|
| `DATABENTO_API_KEY` (or `_FILE`) | Required. |
| `JOBS_STORE` | The store (default `http://127.0.0.1:9120`). |
| `JOBS_LEDGER` | The submissions ledger file (JSON lines, appended); without it nothing can be submitted. The service holds an exclusive lock on `<ledger>.lock` while it runs and refuses to start if another process holds it. Keep it on a local disk, never on a network filesystem shared between hosts: the lock only guards one host. |
| `JOBS_YIELD_TO`, `JOBS_CAPTURE_HOURS`, `JOBS_CALENDAR` | Hold downloads while capture is live (as the [queue](queue.md) does). |
| `JOBS_REFRESH_SECONDS` | How often Databento's job list is read (default 30). |
| `JOBS_APPROVE_TOKEN` (or `_FILE`) | The token a paid purchase's approval carries, shared with the console (`CONSOLE_JOBS_APPROVE_TOKEN`). Unset, paid purchases are refused (`403`); free ones still go through. |

## Endpoints

**A person approves every paid purchase** ([decision 0019](../decisions/0019-purchase-guards.md), with approval in the console instead of a machine-bound key): a quote that costs anything waits (60 minutes, against 15 for a free one) until it is approved or declined in the console's Jobs widget. The console sends `/submit` with the approval token, the login that approved it as `approved_by`, and the price it showed as `confirm_usd`. The ledger records an `approved` step (who, when, the quote, the cost) before each request is sent.

**What's had already** (decision 0019, section 2) is one set, used by pricing and submitting alike, per day, per contract and per time window: the store's Databento holdings (`/coverage`, and any day `/catalog` holds that `/coverage` doesn't explain, as held in full); every job on Databento's list that isn't expired (the list read in full: every state, all history); and every purchase in the ledger, whether or not its job is still listed, including submissions whose outcome isn't known. Job and ledger symbols are parsed: a list naming the parent or `ALL_SYMBOLS` covers it all, contracts of the parent count one by one, and instrument IDs or anything unreadable cover every day they overlap. Every read of the store (`/catalog`, `/coverage`, `/files`) has a 120 s limit. Pricing uses the store's last scan; under the purchase lock, `/submit` re-checks against a fresh one (`fresh=1` on `/catalog` and `/coverage`, which the store waits up to 600 s for; 660 s limit). Copies outside Databento's folder (`provider: null` in the catalog) are never taken as accounted for by `/coverage`: a day with such a copy counts as held in full. Every purchase is refused (`503`) while the store answers anything but `200`, reports `missing_volumes` (or doesn't report them), or Databento's job list or any listed job can't be read. Datasets are compared in Databento's spelling and must be on its dataset list. One purchase runs at a time: `/submit` holds one lock from their last check through the ledger's last write, and reconciliation waits while a purchase is in flight. Every Databento call is bounded (60 s for reads, 120 s for a submission, which is then written `uncertain`). A submission is matched to a job received after its `sending` step (less two minutes). A `not-submitted` that an earlier version wrote on its own counts as unresolved again; the service logs each at startup.

| Endpoint | |
|---|---|
| `GET /jobs` | `{"jobs": [...], "store_reachable", "updated_at"}`. Each job: `id`, `state` (`queued`, `processing`, `done`, `expired`), `dataset`, `schema`, `symbols`, `stype_in`, `start`, `end`, `cost_usd`, `bytes`, `received`, `expires`, `held` (where the store has it), `transfer` (download progress), `unfileable` (why the store would refuse it). Times are integer Unix seconds. |
| `GET /datasets` | `{"datasets": [...]}` |
| `GET /schemas?dataset=` | `{"schemas": [...]}` |
| `POST /price` | `{dataset, schema, symbol, stype_in?, start, end, rule? \| contracts?, hours?, again?}` → a quote (below). `symbol` is a parent (`SPY.OPT`, the whole chain) or a raw symbol; `stype_in` is `parent` (by default for a name with a dot) or `raw_symbol`. Days `start` to `end`, inclusive, at most 1,500. For an option parent only, `rule` (`expiries`: the first N expiries from each day; `dte_max`; `strike_min`/`strike_max` in dollars) or `contracts` (`{"YYYY-MM-DD": ["SPY   221021C00370000", …]}`: exactly these, each of which must be in the store's definitions of that day, `400` otherwise; days outside the range are `400`) buys by contract. `hours` (`{"start": "09:30", "end": "16:01", "tz": "America/New_York"}`, New York only, daylight saving applied per day) narrows each day to those hours. Only what's missing is priced: a day with nothing of the symbol had in its window is bought whole (consecutive whole days as one request; with `hours`, a request per day); of an option parent, a day partly had (or any day, with a rule or contracts) is bought as its missing contracts over the hours each still needs (split at 2,000), from the store's definitions of the day; of any other symbol, a day partly had isn't bought, and a note says why. `again: ["OPRA-…"]` names exactly the expired or no longer listed purchases here whose contracts and days may be bought again (`400` for any other, or for `true`); otherwise they stay subtracted, and the notes name them. Requests are priced four at a time, each on its own Databento client, each given up to 5 minutes and tried up to three times on a 5xx or no answer. `200` with the quote, or `202` with it still `pricing` when that takes over 20 s; a price not done in 4 hours becomes `failed`. Each request's price must be a number of dollars, at least 0: otherwise the quote's `cost_usd` is not a number, it needs approval, and it is never bought. Within a quote no two requests cover the same contracts and time (the plan is refused if they would); New York hours use the offset in force at each end, so whole days tile across the daylight-saving changes. |
| `GET /quotes`, `GET /quotes/{id}` | `{"quotes": [quote, …]}`, or one quote. A quote: `quote` (its ID, `q-<random per start>-<n>`, never repeated across restarts), `state` (`pricing`, `ready`, `failed` with `error`), `priced` and `of` (requests priced so far), `dataset`, `schema`, `symbol`, `stype_in`, `start`, `end`, `hours`, `rule`, `contracts` and `contract_days` (counts, for contract lists), `again`; once ready, `cost_usd`, `billable_bytes`, `needs_approval` (it costs anything), `valid_seconds`, `notes` (what was skipped and why), and `requests`: each `{kind: "whole" (with symbol) \| "contracts" (with contracts, skipped_held, skipped_pending), first_day, last_day, window: [start, end], hours, cost_usd, billable_bytes}`. A window's ends are a date (midnight UTC) or RFC 3339. |
| `DELETE /quotes/{id}` | Declines a quote; needs the approval token (`403` otherwise). → `{"declined"}`. |
| `POST /submit` | `{quote, confirm_usd, approved_by?, again?}` → `{"submitted": [request…, each with its job], "cost_usd"}`. A quote is used once. Plans it again under the purchase lock, against a fresh scan of the store, and submits only if every request and the total are unchanged (`409` otherwise; `409` too when there's nothing to buy). A paid one needs the approval token as its bearer token and `approved_by` (`403`), and `confirm_usd` exactly the price (`428`); either refusal leaves the quote as it was, to be approved again. `again`, when given, must be exactly the quote's. Each request is written to the ledger before it is sent; a failure stops there and lists what was submitted. It runs to its end even if the caller goes away. Jobs of contract lists are filed as partial days of the parent (`/incorporate?parent=`, decision 0018). |
| `POST /ledger/resolve` | `{request, at, job, note}` → `{"request", "state", "job"}`. The operator's word on a submission whose outcome isn't known (its latest ledger step `sending` or `uncertain`, identified by its `request` fingerprint and `at`): `job` is the job it made (written `submitted`; it must be on Databento's list, for the request's dataset and schema, and not another submission's job, `409` otherwise), or `null` for none (`not-submitted`; `409`, saying when it will be allowed, until the submission was sent at least 30 minutes ago and a complete read of the job list begun after then hasn't found it). Nothing else ever writes `not-submitted`. `404` when there's no such unresolved step. |
